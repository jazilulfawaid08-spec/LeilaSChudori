The Story That Stayed With Me

Pulang

Pulang tells the story of Dimas Suryo, an Indonesian journalist who becomes stranded abroad after the political turmoil of 1965. Unable to return to Indonesia, he builds a new life in Paris while carrying memories of his homeland.

The novel explores the impact of political conflict on personal lives and families. Through its rich characters and historical background, Pulang offers readers a deeper understanding of Indonesia's past and the meaning of belonging.
